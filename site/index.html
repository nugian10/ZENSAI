    Agent ID holders mint free through their Binance Agentic Wallet. Everyone else mints the paid trees with any wallet on this page — up to 30 in one transaction. Max 30 per wallet total.

Questions

Who gets the free mint?

Only wallets holding a Binance .ai Agent ID, minted through their agent. Public wallets cannot claim the free mint — the contract enforces it on-chain. One free mint per wallet.

Who can mint the paid trees?

Anyone. Connect any wallet on this page and mint 1 to 30 trees in one transaction. Max 30 per wallet total. No Agent ID needed, no oracle: the page reads your exact price from the contract before you confirm.

Can I mint 30 at once?

Yes — bulk minting is built in. One transaction mints up to 30 trees. If you use your free mint in the same batch, it lands on the most expensive slot in that batch.

What is the most one wallet pays?

Max 30 trees per wallet — 1 free + 29 paid. Paid mints earn 120,000 ZSAI each (max 3,480,000 per wallet).

What is $ZSAI?

ZSAI is the bonus token for paid minters: 120,000 ZSAI per paid mint (max 3,480,000 per wallet). Max supply 1B — 540M in the bonus pool, 460M treasury. It has no price and no liquidity pool yet, and nothing here promises it will ever have one.

When does mint open?

Mint opens when the owner activates it after deployment. The counter above grows with every mint.
